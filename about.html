About us

It began with a fight against slavery.

Created in 2006, Just Business began by cultivating the non-profit Not For Sale in its fight to end human trafficking and labour exploitation.

Our story

Then we started building businesses that could win.

Just Business has continued to invest in and help develop forward-thinking companies dedicated to championing social change.

Our team brings a unique mix of business and social impact acumen, with more than 50 years of collective leadership experience: from dynamic start-ups to Fortune 500 multinationals, in sectors from consumer products and environmental sustainability to engineering, media, fashion and e-commerce.

Together with our international network, we’ve cultivated a focused portfolio of enterprises, designed to strengthen financial and impact outcomes together by prioritising hands-on collaboration over passive investing.
