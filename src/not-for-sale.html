Why two organisations

One fights exploitation. The other out-builds it.

Just Business began in 2006 by cultivating Not For Sale in its fight to end human trafficking and labour exploitation.

Not For Sale works on the ground: supporting people at risk, investigating the systems that profit from exploitation, and restoring the ecosystems whose destruction pushes communities into it.

Just Business builds the other half of the answer: profitable companies with virtuous supply chains and real jobs, so that the market rewards doing right.
